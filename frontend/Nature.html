An Informative Article About Nature

Introduction

Nature makes our planet beautiful and supports all living things. It provides us with fresh air, clean water, food and shelter. Forests, rivers, mountains and oceans are important parts of nature. We must protect the environment from pollution and destruction.

Types of Natural Environments

  • Forests
  • Mountains
  • Rivers
  • Oceans
  • Grasslands
  • Deserts

Information About Nature

Natural Feature Importance
Forests Provide oxygen and shelter for animals
Rivers Supply water for people and wildlife
Mountains Support wildlife and store freshwater
Oceans Support marine life and regulate climate

Save Nature

Plant more trees, reduce plastic waste and keep our surroundings clean. Small actions can help protect our planet.
